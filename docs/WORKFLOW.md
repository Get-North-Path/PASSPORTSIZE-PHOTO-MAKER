# Photo workflows

Typical sequences on [passportsize.getnorthpath.com](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/). Each path links to live tools and guides.

These are helpers, not a guarantee of acceptance. Official specifications win. See [RULES.md](RULES.md).

---

## Make a passport or visa photo (main path)

1. Open [Passport photo maker](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/tools/passport-photo-maker) (or the home [Make my photo](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/) flow)
2. Pick **country** and **document** (62 presets)
3. Drop in a front-facing JPG, PNG, WEBP, or HEIC (up to 25 MB). The file stays in the tab
4. Check the **compliance panel**: chin-to-crown millimetres vs the published range, background shade, glasses rule
5. Optionally replace the background; use straighten / size / position sliders if the crown line is wrong
6. Export **print** (DPI in the JPEG) and/or **digital** (pixels and kilobyte cap)
7. Enter an email to unlock the download

Read: [How it works](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/how-it-works) · [Photo requirements](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/photo-requirements)

Home copy describes this as **four steps, about three minutes**: upload → measure → background → download.

## Wrong background colour

1. [Background remover](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/tools/background-remover), or turn replacement on in the photo maker
2. Pick the shade for that country (white is not always correct — UK grey/cream, Ireland/Germany light grey)
3. Retake the photo if the **face** is shadowed; replacement cannot fix lighting on the subject

Read: [Background colour by country](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/blog/passport-photo-background-colour-by-country)

---

## Existing photo, need an exact crop

1. [Crop to passport size](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/tools/crop-photo-to-passport-size) or [Convert photo to passport size](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/tools/convert-photo-to-passport-size)
2. If the original has no space above the hair, the compliance panel should flag a cut-off crown — take a wider source photo
3. Export print and digital separately if the authority wants both

---

## Portal rejects the file for size (KB)

1. Identify the published window ([Digital passport photo](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/digital-passport-photo))
2. Re-export from the photo maker’s **digital** preset, or run [Resize photo in KB](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/tools/resize-photo-in-kb)
3. For US 240 KB: keep pixel dimensions if possible; quality search first
4. For Chinese 40–120 KB: the product copy describes searching quality down for the ceiling and **padding** if under the floor

Related: [Compress image](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/tools/compress-image) for document scans (IRCC-style ~4 MB caps)

## IRCC online application photo

1. Use the [IRCC digital photo](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/ircc-photo) hub, not the 50×70 mm passport preset
2. Confirm at least **420×540 px**, **JPEG or PNG**, under **4 MB**, head **31–36 mm** at print scale
3. Do not retouch or beautify (IRCC treats altered photos as a misrepresentation risk per site copy)
4. Paper passport applications still need the [Canada passport](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/canada-passport-photo) 50×70 mm prints

## US DS-160 / passport upload

1. [US visa](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/us-visa-photo) or [US passport](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/us-passport-photo) / [2×2 maker](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/2x2-photo-maker)
2. Square 2×2 in, head 1–1⅜ in, eyes 1⅛–1⅜ in from the bottom, glasses off
3. Digital: 600×600 to 1200×1200 px, JPEG, under 240 KB
4. Print a spare 4×6 sheet for the interview even if the upload succeeded

---

## USCIS form photos (I-485, N-400, I-765, I-131)

1. [USCIS photo](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/uscis-photo)
2. Same 2×2 geometry as a US passport photo, but **taken within 30 days of filing**
3. Two identical colour prints per form unless that form’s instructions say otherwise
4. Lightly pencil name / A-number on the back when the form asks

---

## Infant or newborn

1. [Baby passport photo](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/baby-passport-photo)
2. Lay the baby on a plain sheet; photograph from directly above; keep adult hands out of frame
3. Expression and gaze are relaxed for infants; **size, background, and unobstructed face** still apply
4. Use background replacement if the sheet is wrinkled or off-colour

---

## Signature for an Indian form

1. [Signature resize](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/tools/signature-resize)
2. Sign on plain white paper; photograph straight on
3. Pick the preset (for example 140×60 px under 20 KB) and adjust the ink threshold
4. Prefer white JPEG unless the form accepts transparent PNG

---

## Several countries from one sitting

1. Take one high-resolution photo with **generous space** around the head, facing a window, ~2 m from the camera
2. Export separately for each country — do not reuse one output file for a different head-height rule
3. Outer 35×45 mm is shared widely, but UK 29–34 mm vs Ireland 31.5–36 mm still differ
