## Canada vs IRCC (do not mix these up)

Site copy is explicit:

- **Paper Canadian passport:** 50×70 mm prints
- **IRCC online immigration photo:** 35×45 mm ratio delivery with pixel/KB rules; same 31–36 mm face geometry at print scale
