## Requirements guide (summary)

[Photo requirements](https://passportsize.getnorthpath.com/photo-requirements) is the canonical checklist. Four groups:

1. Size and head position
2. Background
3. Face, expression, and eyes
4. Image quality and age

If you only check three things, the page says: **head height in millimetres**, **background shade for that country**, **shadows on wall and face**.
