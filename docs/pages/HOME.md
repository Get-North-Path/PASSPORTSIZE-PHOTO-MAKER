## What you do here

1. Choose **Make my photo, free** or **Find my country**
2. Scan most-requested formats (Canada, IRCC digital, US passport/visa, UK, China, India, Schengen)
3. Follow the four-step explanation (upload → millimetres → background → download)
4. Open one of seven tools or a document hub
5. Read FAQ, then optionally GetNorthPath DIY tools or a product walkthrough

Hero claims verified on 5 October 2026: **no account, no watermark, no upload, 54 countries, print & digital**. Badge: *Runs in your browser · nothing uploaded*.
